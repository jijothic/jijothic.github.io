Overview
Tram Cream Coffee is a quiet station within the rhythm of everyday life. Inspired by the spirit of a transit point, it embraces the beauty of pausing, reconnecting, and preparing for what comes next. Each cup becomes a small ritual of renewal, each visit a moment to gather thoughts before moving forward.

The brand balances warmth and stillness through a restrained visual language, allowing atmosphere, materiality, and emotion to take center stage. More than a café, Tram Cream Coffee is a place where every pause becomes the beginning of something greater.




The Challenge
Tram Cream Coffee set out to become more than just another name in a crowded coffee market. The challenge was to build a living brand identity that people could connect with. Tram had to become an emotional signature that can stand out through a meaningful story and a distinctive presence that customers could recognize, trust, and remember across different locations.

That journey was defined by three key missions:
1. Crafting a meaningful brand story that lays the foundation for enduring love and connection.
2. Designing a visual identity that is both deeply inspiring and highly adaptable across every touchpoint.
3. Executing a holistic brand system with depth, coherence, and long-term sustainability.




Our Strategy
The creative direction for Tram Cream Coffee reimagined the idea of a “station”, not as a physical stop, but as a mental pause. Inspired by transportation architecture, Vietnamese coffee culture, and modern graphic systems, the brand creates a balance between structure and emotion, practicality and poetry. Through visual symbolism and storytelling, Tram becomes a metaphorical space for creativity, connection, and reflection, encouraging people to slow down, recharge, and find inspiration within their everyday routines.

Design System
Visual Language
Rooted in the aesthetics of railway shelters, station signage, rail lines, and the nostalgic essence of Vietnamese coffee culture, the visual system captures the rhythm of travel and the comfort of familiarity.

Symbolic Character
A walking Vietnamese coffee filter serves as the brand’s mascot - a whimsical yet iconic figure embodying the spirit of exploration, optimism, and Vietnamese pride.

Brand Experience
Every touchpoint of the brand is designed as part of a continuous emotional journey, where each customer feels as though they are stepping into a moving story - one of motion, mindfulness, and meaning.





In the end, Tram Cream Coffee is more than a design project. A place where every sensory detail is carefully curated, every emotion honored, and every moment crafted to inspire movement.

It’s where anyone who stops by leaves just a little more ready - ready to continue their own adventure, to reconnect with themselves, or to rediscover the quiet joy of being present. Tram was never meant to stand still. It was made to move with you through cities, through stories, through life itself.
