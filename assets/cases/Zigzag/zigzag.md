No one gets a puppy because it's so much work. You get him because he's so cute! But raising a dog isn't something that happens overnight. It's a process of ups and downs. Zigzag guides you through the entire journey, and makes it fun too. Even when he poops on the carpet for the third time.

We helped Zigzag as an in-house design team with the design of the app for 18 months. In bi-weekly sprints, we made the app slightly better each time, through design choices based on analytics and interviews with users.

We also ensured that the cheerful branding was palpable in every corner of the app

Result
After intensive collaboration with the Zigzag team, we launched the new app with great success. In an extremely competitive market, Zigzag was named App of the Day in the UK App Store and grew to become the #1 puppy training app in the UK, with over 1 million downloads to date. And most importantly: we helped create an unforgettable bond between countless puppies and their owners.
