Employee onboarding and offboarding involve more than adding or removing someone from a system. These processes span departments, tools, permissions, and admin tasks - and when they're fragmented, work becomes repetitive, coordination gets harder, and access can remain in place long after someone leaves.

Handled set out to simplify this by bringing employee lifecycle management into one cohesive platform. The challenge was twofold: build a distinctive brand in a crowded, technically focused market, and translate that identity into a digital product that feels approachable, clear, and easy to understand.

I led most of the design work across the brand, website, and product interface - building a connected experience from the first brand touchpoint through to the platform itself.

Brand idea

The project began with naming and brand strategy. Several names were explored - Fluxfuse, Hendlee, Cierge, Skwit - before landing on Handled: direct, confident, and focused on outcomes rather than technical complexity.

From there, I built a visual identity that deliberately moves away from the conventional look of enterprise software. Instead of restrained corporate palettes and abstract technology imagery, the brand uses bright colors, expressive typography, and a recognizable character named Jimmy - giving the idea of having a process under control a human face.

From brand to product

The platform needed to feel like a natural extension of the brand, not a separate enterprise application. I carried the same visual language - colors, typography, illustration style, and Jimmy himself - into the product interface, so the marketing experience and the product never drift apart.

The design direction balances approachability with the clarity and control expected from a business application: make the experience feel manageable, without making the underlying operational complexity invisible.

Result

Handled launched with a distinctive brand identity, a website, and a product interface built around one shared design direction: a name and positioning centered on simplicity and ownership, a visual identity that stands apart from conventional enterprise software, a website that leads with recognizable customer problems before introducing the solution, and a product interface that extends the brand's visual language into the platform itself. The result is a unified digital presence that communicates the same idea at every stage: onboarding and offboarding should feel handled, not complicated.
