Zigzag

Puppy parenting, guided

Client: Zigzag

Timespan: 18 months

Key Focus: App Design

No one gets a puppy because it's so much work. You get one because it's so cute - but raising a dog isn't something that happens overnight, it's a process of ups and downs. Zigzag guides new owners through the entire journey, and makes it fun too. Even when he poops on the carpet for the third time.

What we did

We helped Zigzag as an in-house design team with the app for 18 months. In bi-weekly sprints, we made the app slightly better each time, through design choices grounded in analytics and interviews with real users. We also made sure the cheerful branding was palpable in every corner of the app - not just the splash screen.

Cheerful branding, everywhere

Raising a puppy is stressful enough without an app that feels clinical. Every screen, from empty states to error messages, was designed to feel like a friendly voice cheering the owner on - warm illustration, playful copy, and a design system built to scale as the product grew.

A daily training plan that adapts to the puppy's age and breed, and a progress tracker that turns small wins - a clean walk, a full night's sleep - into visible momentum.

A walkthrough of the day-to-day flow - from the first day home to the first trick landed - designed so new owners never feel lost about what to do next.

Result

After intensive collaboration with the Zigzag team, we launched the new app with great success. In an extremely competitive market, Zigzag was named App of the Day in the UK App Store and grew to become the #1 puppy training app in the UK, with over 1 million downloads to date. And most importantly: we helped create an unforgettable bond between countless puppies and their owners.
