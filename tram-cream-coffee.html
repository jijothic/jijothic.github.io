Overview

Tram Cream Coffee is a quiet station within the rhythm of everyday life. Inspired by the spirit of a transit point, it embraces the beauty of pausing, reconnecting, and preparing for what comes next.

Each cup becomes a small ritual of renewal; each visit, a moment to gather thoughts before moving forward. The identity balances warmth and stillness through a restrained visual language that lets atmosphere, materiality, and emotion take centre stage.

Brand concept & story Logo design Visual identity Packaging design Website design

The challenge

Tram Cream Coffee needed to become more than another name in a crowded coffee market. The task was to build a living identity people could connect with: an emotional signature with a meaningful story and a distinctive presence that customers could recognise, trust, and remember across locations.

01 — Make meaning

Craft a brand story that lays the foundation for enduring love and connection.

02 — Build flexibility

Design a visual identity that can inspire while adapting across every touchpoint.

03 — Create coherence

Execute a holistic system with depth, consistency, and long-term sustainability.

“A station, not as a physical stop, but as a mental pause.”Tram Cream Coffee creative direction

The strategy

The creative direction reimagines the station as a place for creativity, connection, and reflection. Transportation architecture, Vietnamese coffee culture, and modern graphic systems meet to balance structure with emotion, practicality with poetry.

Design system

The visual language takes its cues from railway shelters, station signage, rail lines, and the nostalgia of Vietnamese coffee culture. Graphic patterns carry the rhythm of travel, while the palette makes the system feel familiar and inviting.

Brand experience

A walking Vietnamese coffee filter becomes the brand’s optimistic guide: a character for exploration, Vietnamese pride, and the momentum of daily life. From packaging to street-level signage, every touchpoint forms part of a continuous emotional journey.

The result

Tram Cream Coffee is more than a design project—it is a place where every pause becomes the beginning of something greater.

The resulting system brings consistency to the brand’s story, visual language, packaging, and future customer experiences. It is designed to move with people through cities, stories, and the quiet joy of being present.
